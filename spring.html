Spring Tips

The European Health Insurance card (EHIC) is a free card that allows you to access medically necessary state-provided healthcare while you are staying in another european country
To stay warm its recommended to wear warm & waterproof clothing as it can still get quite cold during the spring! Being on the move actively ensures that you stay warm. Sweating and staying still with alot of clothing can actually get you cold.
Locals are happy to help! So ask locals if you are confused and need help with something!
Bring good walking shoes and breathable clothing as you can expect to walk around alot.
