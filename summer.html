Summer Activities

Discover Summer in Full Bloom

Here you can discover some summer activities to enjoy the warm season.

Outdoor Adventures

Summer is perfect for exploring nature. You can go for long walks, cycle through green areas, visit parks, or spend time near the water. Both Helmond and Rovaniemi have beautiful outdoor spaces where you can enjoy the fresh air.

Culture & History

Discover local culture by visiting museums, historical buildings, and city landmarks. Helmond is known for Kasteel Helmond, while Rovaniemi offers museums like Arktikum, where you can learn about Lapland and Arctic life.

Relaxing Days

Not every summer day has to be busy. You can relax at a café, have a picnic, read outside, or enjoy ice cream in the city centre. These slower activities are great for warm summer days.

Helmond & Rovaniemi

Both cities are great places to enjoy summer. Hover over each card to discover how you can enjoy swimming, canoeing, and outdoor terraces in both destinations.

Helmond

A relaxed summer city with canals, parks, cafés, and calm outdoor spots.

  • Swimming at local pools or nearby recreational water areas
  • Canoeing along canals and peaceful water routes
  • Enjoying outdoor terraces in the city centre

Rovaniemi

A northern summer destination with rivers, nature, long daylight, and fresh air.

  • Swimming in lakes, rivers, or outdoor summer spots
  • Canoeing on calm rivers surrounded by nature
  • Relaxing on outdoor terraces during bright summer evenings

Why Visit in Summer?

Summer gives both cities a completely different atmosphere. Helmond becomes lively with terraces, green parks, and outdoor events, while Rovaniemi offers long bright days, peaceful nature, and unique northern experiences. Whether you enjoy culture, nature, or relaxing with friends, both destinations have something special to offer.

Summer Travel Tips

Bring comfortable walking shoes, because many summer activities involve walking through parks, city centres, museums, or nature areas.
Pack layers. Even in summer, the weather can change quickly, especially in Rovaniemi.
Use sunscreen and stay hydrated when spending a lot of time outside.
Check opening times before visiting museums, castles, or tourist attractions.
Bring your European Health Insurance Card when travelling within Europe.
